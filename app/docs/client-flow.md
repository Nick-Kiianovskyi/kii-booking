# KII Booking - Client Flow

## Product Purpose

Allow a client to book an appointment quickly and easily.

No registration is required.

The client should be able to complete the booking process in less than one minute.

---

# Client User Journey

## Step 1. Receive Booking Link

The client receives a booking link from the business.

Examples:

- Telegram

- Viber

- WhatsApp

- Instagram

- Email

- Website

Example link:

https://kii-booking.com/book/nikolay-barber

Result:

Client opens booking page.

---

## Step 2. View Booking Page

The booking page displays:

- Business Name

- Booking calendar

- Available appointment slots

The page must be simple and easy to understand.

---

## Step 3. Select Date

The client selects a preferred date.

Only dates with available appointment slots should be selectable.

Result:

Available appointment times are loaded.

---

## Step 4. Select Time Slot

The system displays only available appointment slots.

Example:

09:00

09:30

10:00

11:30

15:00

Unavailable slots must not be displayed.

Result:

Client chooses a convenient time.

---

## Step 5. Enter Contact Information

Required fields:

- Name

- Phone

- Service

The form should contain only the minimum amount of information required.

---

## Step 6. Create Booking

Client clicks:

Book Appointment

Result:

Booking request is submitted.

---

## Step 7. Booking Confirmation

Client sees confirmation screen.

Example:

✅ Appointment Confirmed

Business:

Nikolay Barber

Date:

25 Nov 2026

Time:

11:30

Result:

Client knows booking was successful.

---

# Design Principles

The booking process must be:

- simple

- fast

- mobile friendly

- easy to understand

- focused on appointment creation

The booking page should resemble modern scheduling products such as Calendly.

Avoid:

- registration

- login

- dashboards

- complex forms

- unnecessary settings

- multiple screens

---

# Mobile Experience

Most clients will use smartphones.

Mobile experience is the primary design target.

Flow:

Calendar

↓

Available Slots

↓

Booking Form

↓

Confirmation

---

# Desktop Experience

Desktop layout may show:

Left:

Calendar

Right:

Available Slots

After slot selection:

Booking Form

---

# Out Of Scope

The following features are NOT part of MVP:

- Client registration

- Client account

- Appointment editing

- Appointment cancellation

- Appointment history

- Notifications center

- Client dashboard

---

# Success Criteria

A client can:

✅ Open booking link

✅ Select date

✅ View available slots

✅ Select preferred slot

✅ Enter contact information

✅ Submit booking

✅ Receive booking confirmation

✅ Complete the entire process in less than one minute
